# Material photography — sources and licences

Stand-in photographs of the materials in the catalogue, used when a vendor has
not uploaded photos of their own listing. A vendor's uploads always take
precedence; see `src/constants/materialImages.js` for how a listing is matched
to a file.

All files come from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org) and were
downloaded on 2026-08-07. Each was centre-cropped to 3:2 and resized to
1200×800 JPEG; no other alteration was made. The CC BY and CC BY-SA files below
require the credit given here to be kept — the material detail page shows it
under the image.
